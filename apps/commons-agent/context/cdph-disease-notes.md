# Condition-specific notes from the CDPH data dictionary

## Case definitions

Counts include cases that met the surveillance case definition for each disease. National surveillance case definitions are published by CDC at http://wwwn.cdc.gov/nndss/case-definitions.html.

## Coccidioidomycosis (Valley Fever)

Coccidioidomycosis counts include only "Confirmed" cases. They can be lower than the combined Suspect, Probable, and Confirmed counts published in CDPH's "Coccidioidomycosis in California Provisional Monthly Report."

## Diseases with later reporting start dates

Compare years before these start dates with caution:

- Shiga toxin-producing E. coli (STEC) non-O157 serogroups: required reporting began in 2006. Both STEC diseases (with and without HUS) include O157 and non-O157 serogroups.
- Creutzfeldt-Jakob Disease and other Transmissible Spongiform Encephalopathies: required reporting began in 2007.
- Cyclosporiasis and Hepatitis E, acute infection: required reporting began in 2011.
- Chikungunya Virus Infection, Flavivirus Infection of Undetermined Species, and Zika Virus Infection: required reporting began June 1, 2016, so rates and confidence intervals were not calculated for 2016.

## Disease groupings

- Shigellosis includes subtypes A through D and unspecified.
- Listeriosis includes neonatal and non-neonatal cases.
- Hemolytic Uremic Syndrome includes all reported HUS cases, with and without evidence of STEC.

## Rates and confidence intervals

- Rates use California Department of Finance population estimates: P-3 projection tables for 2010-2023 and E-2 estimate tables for 2001-2009.
- 95% confidence intervals use the exact (Clopper-Pearson) method.
- Rows CDPH suppressed for small numbers follow the DHCS Data De-Identification Guidelines (Publication Scoring Criteria).

## Onset year

The year is based on the estimated onset date: the date closest to when symptoms first appeared. When onset is not recorded, the estimate can fall anywhere from symptom onset to the date of the report to CDPH.
